Support

What does ByteFix Tech sell?

We sell gaming laptops, school laptops, and workstations.

Do you offer repair and maintenance?

Yes, send us a message using the contact support form and choose "Repair" as the topic. Tell us what is wrong with your device and we will get back to you.

How do I pay for my order?

We accept cash on delivery. Add your items to the cart, click checkout, fill out the delivery form, and pay in cash when your order arrives.

How do I choose the right laptop?

Send us your budget and what you will use the laptop for, such as gaming, schoolwork, editing, or office work. Choose "buying inquiries" as the topic and we will recommend a good fit.

Warranty

Every device sold by ByteFix Tech is tested before it reaches you, and we will support you after your purchase.

Warranty period: 3 months from the date of purchase.

What is covered

  • Manufacturing defects and faulty parts.
  • Hardware problems that were not caused by misuse.
  • Repair or replacement of the affected part.

What is not covered

  • Physical damage, such as cracked screens or drops.
  • Liquid damage.
  • Damage from repairs done by other shops.
  • Software problems and viruses.

How to make a claim

  • Keep your receipt or proof of purchase.
  • Use the contact support form and choose "Warranty" as the topic.
  • Tell us what is wrong with the device and we will guide you.
